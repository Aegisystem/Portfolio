## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a drawing set: each project is a sheet, dimensioned with measured numbers and stamped with an honest status. It refuses the dev-portfolio default of a dark hero, bento cards and tech icon tiles.

OWN-WORLD: Bond-white sheets (#f3f5f4) on a green self-healing cutting mat with its grid; diazo blue-violet ink (#2b2f8f) for every line and letter; checker's yellow highlighter only on verified figures; redline red only on superseded values and revision clouds. ISO 3098 lettering (osifont) for titles, labels, title blocks and dimensions; Archivo for running notes. Thick frame line, thin cell rules, zone markers 1–8 / A–F, detail bubbles, title blocks, revision tables, BOM tables, hatching. No cards, pills, gradients or glows.

STORY: The visitor sees the name, role and five projects with measured proof in the first viewport, follows a detail bubble into a project sheet (architecture drawing, parts list, notes, links), reads the career as a revision table, and leaves by writing an email or taking the CV.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sheet 00 fills the viewport on the mat with a 26px margin and a right-hand sheet rail (00–06). Inside the frame: main field left with "STEVEN CORREA" at 96px lettering, role line and thesis; below, the sheet index (rows 01–06: bubble, name, one-liner, dimension drawing, status). Right vertical title block 316px: author, location/UTC−5, primary action "Escribir un correo" as the ink-filled cell plus LinkedIn/GitHub/CV, revisions table, ES/EN switch, status/sheet number. Signature interaction: detail bubbles and index rows are links that jump to their sheet; on arrival the sheet's linework plots in (stroke draw) and its title block inks to full while other sheets stay at underlay strength; hovering a BOM balloon highlights its parts-list row with the yellow highlighter. Motion grammar: pen-plotter draw-in once per sheet, highlighter swipe left to right, nothing else; all static under reduced motion.

FORM: Engineering drawing set (ISO 5457 frames, ISO 7200 title blocks), position 5 of 7 on the grounded list; seed seed:18961eccc0090701.
